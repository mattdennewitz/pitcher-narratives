<objective>
Add per-pitch-type horizontal movement (pfx_x) and vertical movement (pfx_z)
YoY deltas to PitchTrend dataclass and wire them through context rendering
and specialist pipelines.

Purpose: Movement shape changes are one of the strongest signals a scout looks
for year-over-year. A pitcher who lost 2 inches of vertical break on his slider
is a different pitcher -- the LLM needs this information alongside the existing
velo/usage/P+/S+ deltas.

Output: PitchTrend carries pfx_x/pfx_z deltas, the YoY section renders them
for non-Steady pitches, and the stuff + game shape specialists see movement
changes in their context.
</objective>
