Where they diverge that they shouldn't

Fact fork — the same number computed in up to three places

"Velo delta vs season", "P+ delta vs season", "usage shift", "a pitcher's season baseline" each exist in A and B (and C) as independent code with different thresholds & rounding: scout._check_velo_delta / _check_pplus_swing / _check_usage_shifts vs the engine's arsenal-trend deltas; digest.build_story_cue:73–92 vs engine.compute_league_baselines.

Consequence: the same pitcher & outing rests on a different fact substrate depending on the output. Only A carries the anti-hallucination grounding (outlier_tag, S-variant baselines) the specialist prompts depend on — so morning capsules are written on the thinner, ungrounded substrate.

Voice fork — one "sabermetric scout" voice maintained in four copies

The banned-word list (degradation, binary, profiles as, dominant, elite, massive spike) is duplicated verbatim in personas.py and analyst.py. "Directional consistency", "find the thread", "temporal grounding" recur across personas.py, analyst.py, pipeline.py, prompt_builder.py. Editing the voice means chasing 4–6 files; they will drift.

Already shared — keep & extend

Report and Ask-pipeline run the identical specialists → audit → signal-extractor spine (dashed). That spine is the right shared bone. The fix is to make the three terminal renderers the only divergence, and drive them from one voice composition.

One fact engine → one analysis spine → three thin terminal renderers, each = (persona × output-contract) over one shared voice base.

Unify the voice layer
medium riskone base, persona × contract

Introduce OutputContract (length_target + structure/format rules) as a sibling to Persona (tone/vocab). Move all analytical rules — banned words, directional consistency, temporal grounding, find-the-thread — into SHARED_WRITER_BASE as the single source.

  • Define contracts: CAPSULE (2–3 ¶), DIGEST_ITEM (150–250w), ANSWER (1–3 ¶, no preamble). Length targets migrate off Persona.length_target onto the contract; persona keeps only tone+vocab.
  • build_system_prompt(persona, contract) = base + persona-overlay-chain + contract. Replaces build_writer_system_prompt and digest._build_writer_prompt.
  • Repoint report writer, morning writer, and the ask answerer to it. Ask's tool-calling agent keeps its mechanics (tool use, data-grounding rules) but its voice rules come from the base via the ANSWER contract.
  • Delete V2 voice text + _PRECEDENCE_RULE, V3 voice rules, and the duplicated banned-word/directional blocks.

Unify the fact layer
high riskPitcherContext is the only source

The biggest piece: morning stops computing its own facts. Extract the shared delta math into the engine and have scout/digest consume it.

  • Lift velo-delta, P+-delta, usage-shift, S+/L+-divergence into single engine functions. scout.py becomes pure thresholding/scoring over engine outputs — it keeps its weights & signal taxonomy but stops re-reading aggs with parallel thresholds.
  • Replace build_story_cue's computation (C) with a projection/renderer over PitcherContext (the subset the cue needs) — no independent baseline math.
  • Design risk to resolve first: morning scores many pitchers; PitcherContext is per-pitcher and heavier than B's bulk scan. Decide the loading strategy — keep the cheap bulk scan for triage, then build full PitcherContext only for the ~N selected picks (lazy, post-selection). This preserves morning's cost profile while putting the written picks on substrate A.
